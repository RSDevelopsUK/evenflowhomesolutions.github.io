The Essential EPC

For anyone selling, buying or renting.

  • Legally valid Energy Performance Certificate (A–G rating)
  • RdSAP energy assessment
  • Improvement recommendations report

Right for: sellers, landlords and buyers wanting a baseline.

The Full Picture

For anyone planning improvements or purchasing a property.

A complete snapshot of your home as it stands today:

  • Everything in The Essential
  • Measured floor plan
  • Condition report: damp, mould and structural concerns noted
  • Occupancy assessment: how you use your home affects recommendations
  • Ventilation survey (coming soon)

Right for: new buyers, homeowners before renovation and anyone unsure where to start.

Bill Health Check

For anyone who wants to know they're not overpaying.

A clear, independent review of your current energy bills, with plain-English findings and clear next steps.

Includes: review of your current tariff against market rates using Ofgem-approved comparison tools, energy efficiency measures and behaviour changes.

Right for: anyone with high bills, anyone who has not reviewed their tariff recently, and homeowners who have made improvements but have not seen their bills drop.
