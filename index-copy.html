EPC process: all 6 steps

  1. Book Your Assessment. We arrange a convenient time to visit your property. Assessments typically take 30–60 minutes depending on the size and complexity of the home.
  2. On-Site Survey. A qualified assessor inspects the property — walls, windows, roof, heating system, insulation, and lighting — recording the details that determine energy performance.
  3. Data Analysis. The information is run through approved government software (RdSAP) to calculate the property's energy efficiency and environmental impact ratings.
  4. EPC Generated. You receive an official certificate rating the property from A (most efficient) to G (least efficient), valid for 10 years.
  5. Recommendations Report. Alongside the rating, we provide a clear list of improvements — such as insulation or heating upgrades — with their likely impact on efficiency and running costs.
  6. Lodged on the Register. The EPC is registered on the official government database, satisfying legal requirements for selling, letting, or compliance purposes.
