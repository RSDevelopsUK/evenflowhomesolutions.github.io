Why we are different?

Advice first. Products second.

Renovating and upgrading a home is usually fragmented, slow and confusing. We exist to fix that — putting you at the centre of every decision, not the trades.

Independent by design

We don't sell insulation, boilers or solar panels. Our job is to give you honest, unbiased advice so every decision is yours to make with confidence.

A trusted partner network

When it's time to build, we introduce you to vetted architects, engineers and installers suited to your project — you're always free to choose someone else.

One coherent plan

Instead of juggling separate specialists who don't talk to each other, we bring everything together into a single Home Plan built around your budget and goals

Four stages, start to finish

From your first phone call to living comfortably in a lower-bill home, we guide you through every stage — and we don't disappear at handover.

Four stages, start to finish

01 Concept — Understanding you and your home

A free initial call to hear your goals, followed by an in-depth on-site assessment — energy performance, condition, ventilation and more — giving us a clear picture of where you stand today.

02 Definition — A practical, deliverable plan

Using our expertise and partner network, we turn findings into a ready-to-build plan: options, costs, timelines, technical requirements, and any planning or building regulation submissions.

03 Delivery — Coordinating trusted specialists

We connect you with vetted installation partners and act as your project sponsor, overseeing quality, coordination, compliance and certification throughout.

04 Transition — Making sure it works for you

We help you understand your home's new systems, then review performance after completion to confirm the improvements are delivering real comfort, efficiency and value.

What the on-site assessment covers

A full picture before we recommend anything

Energy Consumption

We go through your current bills together, explain what's driving them, and find where the savings are.

Energy Performance

A government-standard assessment of your home's fabric, producing an EPC where needed to guide improvements.

Floor Plans

A 3D digital model of your home that can be shared directly with architects for redesigns and new layouts.

Condition Assessment

We inspect the existing building for defects and opportunities, using thermal imaging to bring issues to light.

Ventilation

Airflow is checked carefully — vital for comfort, and for protecting against damp and mould.

Character & Significance

For period properties, we make sure any work respects existing features and stays in keeping with planning requirements.
